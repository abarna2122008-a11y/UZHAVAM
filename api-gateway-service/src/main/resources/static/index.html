Soil-test safety and honest limits

Home tests are screening only. Enter the method and units, and confirm important nutrient or salinity decisions with a recognized soil laboratory or local agriculture extension officer. Do not use home tests to calculate fertilizer or pesticide doses. Potassium is included only for manual entry from a laboratory report; this app provides no home potassium test.

Never use hazardous laboratory reagents at home. Keep household materials and samples away from children and food. The shop links are official directories, not a verified live list of nearby vendors or prices.
